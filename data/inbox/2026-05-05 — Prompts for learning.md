### ❶. Understand Anything Like a Genius 📝

\- I use this when I want depth, not surface-level explanations. The goal is to see the idea from multiple angles until it clicks mentally. (I always use this before I start a deep-dive brief. It solves the “surface-level” problem where your writing feels thin.)

It gives you a summary of the nuances most people miss, then tests if you actually understood it.

### Mini-Prompt:

```markup
“I want to understand [insert topic] as if I were a genius. Break down the concept using advanced analogies, real-world applications, counterexamples, and multiple perspectives, then test my understanding with expert-level questions.”
```

### Pro Secrets:

Don't just read the output.

Answer the “expert-level questions” the AI gives you.

This forces active recall.  
\- Common Mistakes to Avoid: Don't let the AI use "toddler" analogies. If it feels too simple, use the modifier below.

\* Add this → modifier;

```markup
“Make the analogies technical and industry-specific.”
```

#### \# Why It’s a Game-Changer:

• Before: 2 hours of reading articles.  
• After: A 360-degree understanding in 60 seconds.

### ❷. Master Any Skill for FREE ✨

\- Writers are often "accidental" entrepreneurs or researchers. We have to learn SEO, marketing, or layout on the fly. This prompt builds you a masterclass for $0.  
**Mini-Prompt:**

```markup
"Assume you're a master of [insert skill] with 20+ years of experience. Reverse engineer the process that got you there, and build me a day-by-day plan to reach that level as fast as humanly possible using only free or low-cost resources."
```

**Secret hack:**

\- **Input:** Be specific.

Instead of “Marketing,” use “Email Marketing for Substack Authors.”

- **Output:** A structured, chronological roadmap with resource links.

#Advanced Tips: ➠ If you have a specific deadline?

Add this →

```markup
“Condense this into a 14-day ‘hell week’ schedule.”
```

### ❸. Fix Mental Blocks 🚀

> This is for the days when my cursor just blinks. Work is stalled, but motivation isn’t the issue. I want root causes and a system, without the pep talk.

**Mini-Prompt:**

```markup
“I’ve been struggling with [insert personal issue: procrastination, burnout, fear, etc.]. Analyze it like a cognitive scientist. Identify the root causes, the behavioural patterns behind it, and design a habit loop to eliminate it.”
```
- **Pro Secrets:**

Be brutally honest in the #\[insert\] section.

The more context you give (e.g., "i procrastinate in editing and proofreading"), the better the "habit loop" design.  
  
**#Next-Level Moves:  
**Instead of just "trying harder," you get a psychological blueprint to bypass your own resistance.

---

### ❹. Turn Confusion into Clarity 💡

\- I use this when something feels fuzzy even after reading multiple times. The goal is permanent understanding, not temporary recall.  
**Mini-Prompt:**

```markup
"I don't understand [insert complex concept]. Break it down step-by-step using metaphors, visual imagery, and real-world examples. Then create a mental shortcut or framework I can use to remember it forever."
```

\- **Rule of thumb:** Look for the “Mental Shortcut” section—this is usually a goldmine for a catchy section header in your own writing.

**\# Common Pitfalls:**

Avoid letting the AI use the same old “autocomplete” metaphor.

**#Add this**

```markup
→ “Use a metaphor related to [your specific niche, e.g., cooking or space travel].”
```
