## 1\. How to access ‘Claude Computer’.

The only way to access it:

1. Mac only *(soon Windows, stay tuned)*.
2. Download the desktop app **[claude.ai/download](https://claude.ai/download)**.
3. Use a **personal** plan, not a business one *(I guess it’s too much compliance)*.
4. Upgrade your account to either **Pro** ($20) or **Max** ($100). It’s worth it.
5. Go to your **Settings** (bottom left), and turn on these features:

> ***UPDATE**: They now made Claude Computer available for teams.*

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!TOat!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7808cc7c-8020-4369-aa36-89f2567fe6da_3456x2170.png)

Click on your name (bottom left) and then on Settings.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4Epo!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc9177d43-d022-4b56-b879-01400edb1e57_3456x2170.png)

Go to Desktop app > General > Turn on Browser use Computer use.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!KSZ5!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe5313f86-aa06-4f54-82f1-f2922c6c52d8_3456x2170.png)

When toggling on Computer use, you must accept by clicking Turn on.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!RKrO!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F909c7523-5e4b-4398-ad6d-f0b9cb11c2fd_3456x2170.png)

## 2\. My favorite use cases of ‘Claude Computer’.

A reminder on what’s needed to use Claude Computer, with your phone:

1. You must have the Claude desktop app + a paid personal account.
2. Your computer must always be turned on. That’s why people buy Mac minis.
3. Go to your phone. Open the **Claude** app. Go to **Dispatch**. Ask for something:

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!lqMk!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa5df529b-e011-415a-802e-5687de02bfce_1206x2622.png)

My first prompt: go to my dashboard. Give me some data.

Now let’s do some harder tasks:

#### Find a freelancer for me.

Prompt:

```markup
Go to my Fiverr account on Chrome. Post a job offer for [task]. Then, send a message to the best 10 [job needed] for [task]. Check if I have any unanswered DM, and follow up until one accepts the [task] for [budget] maximum.
```

Prompt:

## 3\. How to schedule tasks with Claude.

That was the obvious next step: scheduling a task with Claude.

Just like… an employee reporting to you.

1. Go to the left sidebar, and look for **Scheduled**.
2. Create a **New task**. Give it a name and description.
3. The most important part: the prompt. Test it before.
4. Choose a frequency. And make sure to select the right model & folder.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!DcRg!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd174044c-56a6-4963-ad27-c944c4439419_3456x2174.png)

Start here.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!RdYo!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F025a2830-5f77-4693-b459-f5dfb7ccc6c1_3456x2168.png)
